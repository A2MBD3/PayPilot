# Security Policy

PayPilot handles merchant payment data, so security reports are taken
seriously and reviewed with high priority.

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you discover a security issue in the PayPilot app or any related
service, please report it **privately** — do not open a public issue.

- **Email:** aam.abdullah1@hotmail.com
- **Telegram:** [t.me/a2mbd3](https://t.me/a2mbd3)

Please include as much detail as you can: affected version, device,
steps to reproduce, and any proof of concept. You will get a response
as soon as possible.

## Scope

- PayPilot Android application (official APKs from this repository)
- PayPilot license activation and update mechanism

Out of scope: social engineering, attacks requiring a stolen device,
and issues in third-party services not operated by PayPilot.

## Safe downloads

Official PayPilot APKs are published **only** on this repository's
[Releases page](https://github.com/A2MBD3/PayPilot/releases). Every
release is digitally signed and published with its SHA-256 checksum.
APKs from any other source may be tampered with — do not install them.
